Who we are

BS.Haus is a creative studio based in Haggerston, London. We partner with founders and leaders to build and design 0→1 experiences across fashion, art, technology and contemporary culture.

The studio is run by Ben Laing and Sarah Stoutamire (BS), a husband and wife creative duo with over a decade of industry experience, collaborating with both start-up and enterprise-level organisations — from early-stage Web3 platforms to global brands like IKEA, Figma, Decathlon and Zalando.

We combine editorial-focused creative direction with systems and stories to build powerful, immersive brand worlds — and we stay with our partners from first idea to MVP and beyond.
