BS.Haus — creative studio

A creative studio in London

BS.Haus is a creative studio, based in London, that partners with founders & leaders to build & design 0→1 experiences across fashion, art, technology and contemporary culture.

The studio is run by Ben Laing and Sarah Stoutamire (BS), a husband and wife creative duo with over a decade of industry experience, collaborating with both start-up and enterprise-level organisations — from early-stage Web3 platforms to global brands like IKEA, Figma, Decathlon and Zalando.

We combine editorial-focused creative direction with systems and stories to build powerful, immersive brand worlds — and we stay with our partners from first idea to MVP and beyond.

How we work

  1. Discover

    Understand

    We begin by challenging assumptions and immersing ourselves in the context. Through research and observation, we uncover the deeper, often unspoken problems.

  2. Define

    Focus

    We synthesise insights and reframe the brief with strategic clarity. This ensures we’re solving the right problem — not just responding to a request.

  3. Develop

    Explore

    We explore multiple directions, pressure-test ideas, and refine collaboratively. The goal is to push creative boundaries while staying grounded in purpose.

  4. Deliver

    Refine

    We execute with precision and intent, translating strategy into tangible outcomes. Every detail is considered to ensure the work lands with lasting impact.
