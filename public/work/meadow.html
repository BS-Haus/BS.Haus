A living system — the identity for a focus brand built around Micro and Macro.Meadow

Client
Meadow
Industry
Wellness, Consumer
Services
Brand Identity, Symbol System, Art Direction, Packaging, Social
Year
2025

Meadow is an ecosystem — the culture, the community, the conversation — built around two products, Micro and Macro, that help you focus inward or expand outward.

Micro is for deep focus, balance and clarity; Macro is for perspective and reflection.

What we did

We built the identity around the idea of a living system: calm and simple above ground, with a network of connection and growth below.

The system spans the wordmark and symbol, fisheye campaign photography, app icons, a brushed-metal tin, holographic and kraft packaging, Macro/Micro posters and social.
