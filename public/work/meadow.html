A living system — a calm, design-led wellness brand built around Micro and Macro.Meadow

Client
Meadow
Industry
Wellness, Consumer
Services
Brand Identity, Symbol System, Art Direction, Packaging, Social
Year
2025

Meadow is a Lisbon-based brand bringing calm, science-informed microdosing to people across Europe, built around two kits: Micro, for focus and clarity, and Macro, for perspective and reflection.

The brief was to stand apart from a category that feels either spiritual or synthetic — to be a gentle guide, not a guru — for design-minded professionals aged 25–40, with the ambition of becoming Europe’s most trusted name in the space.

What we did

We built the identity around a living system: calm, open and simple above ground, with a vast network of connection and growth below — expressed through the wordmark, symbol and a fisheye photographic world.

The system runs through the pill packs and compact, sleeves and embossed boxes, kraft mailers, field notes, the app and social, giving Meadow a quiet confidence from first glance to the product in hand.
