Client
Figma
Industry
Technology, Software, Art
Services
Presentation Design, Template Design, Collaboration

For the launch of Figma Slides, BS were asked to collaborate and launch a new template for the community, showcasing the power of the tool and its potential applications.

What we did

We created The Infinite Scroll — an ancient-manuscript inspired template with embossed parchment, chrome scroll handles and built-in text effects.

Slides push seamlessly into one another, so a deck becomes a single continuous story: add as many slides as you need, end with your logo or a short message — or keep going, forever.
