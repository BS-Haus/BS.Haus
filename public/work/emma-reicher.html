Year

Emma Reicher is a psychotherapist, group analyst and founder of Field Therapy, weaving the integrity of psychotherapy with the intelligence of maturation and the vitality of elemental Chi Kung.

Her work speaks to people who sense there is more to reality than thought — therapists, creatives, leaders and healers. She needed a brand that could hold that depth without feeling esoteric.

What we did

We created a brand world around The Totem — a family of sculptural, shape-shifting forms rendered in many materials — with an elemental colour system of Wood, Water, Earth, Energy, Fire and Psychic.

It comes to life on emmareicher.com: frosted-glass panels over open landscapes, programme “portals” such as Dreams Decoded and Vision Quest, plus brochures, Instagram templates for the Chi Kung Collective and a case film.
