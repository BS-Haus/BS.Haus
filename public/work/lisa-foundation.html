Collective ownership of blue-chip art — from as little as $1.LISA

Client
LISA Foundation
Industry
Web3, Blockchain, Art, Technology
Services
Creative Direction, Product Design, UX & Strategy

LISA Foundation is the first platform for collective ownership and governance of physical art. Using blockchain, LISA utilises fractional ownership to allow investors to purchase physical blue-chip artworks from as little as $1.

Breaking into the art market is hard for beginner collectors: waitlists last for years, the best deals happen behind closed doors and maintenance fees are high. LISA set out to open it up.

What we did

For 2+ years BS led product and digital design at LISA Foundation, helping them scale from idea to MVP and beyond.

We shaped the end-to-end collector experience — curated discovery, fractional purchase and ownership — across web and mobile, alongside the tokenomics and customer-journey mapping that underpins the platform.
