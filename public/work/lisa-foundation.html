Client
LISA Foundation
Industry
Web3, Blockchain, Art, Technology
Services
Creative Direction, Product Design, UX & Strategy

LISA Foundation is the first web3 platform for collective ownership and governance of physical art: investors buy into blue-chip works from as little as $1.

The art market is hard to break into — waitlists last years, the best deals happen behind closed doors and incumbents charge heavy fees. LISA needed a product that made owning art feel open, trustworthy and simple.

What we did

For over two years we led product and digital design, taking LISA from idea to MVP and beyond: curated discovery, fractional purchase and the ownership experience across web and mobile, underpinned by tokenomics and customer-journey mapping.

In closed beta, two of the first three releases sold out — $105k in sales across 257 collectors. The launch campaign centred on Magdalena Abakanowicz’s Walking Figures with the Vancouver Biennale.
