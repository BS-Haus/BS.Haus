Emma Reicher is a Chi Kung teacher and BodyMind practitioner whose work speaks to people who sense there is more to reality than thought — an invitation to outgrow your relationship to your mind.

What we did

We built a brand world around The Totem: a family of sculptural, shape-shifting forms rendered in many materials, paired with an elemental colour system — Wood, Water, Earth, Metal and Fire.

The identity extends into a website of frosted-glass panels over open landscapes, programme “portals” such as Dreams Decoded and Vision Quest, brochures and Instagram templates for the Chi Kung Collective.
