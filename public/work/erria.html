0→1 Brand & Product Design

Erria is a platform built to support creative work and the people behind it, connecting buyers directly with independent makers and designers.

As they prepared to scale, Erria asked us to help translate their mission of transparency, equity and community into a sharper brand identity and digital presence that could drive growth while staying true to their values.

What we did

BS led the full creative direction for this evolution, delivering a refreshed brand system and modular identity that works seamlessly across social media, storefront and packaging.

We designed and launched Erria 2.0, a brand new e-commerce marketplace that creates a more intentional sales ecosystem between buyers and sellers. Alongside this, we developed a new landing page to articulate their mission and showcase products with clarity.

The result is a cohesive brand and practical toolkit that empowers Erria to communicate their purpose while making it easier for buyers to discover, shop and collect meaningfully.
