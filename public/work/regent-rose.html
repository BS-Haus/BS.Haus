A day-and-night skincare brand, built from the wordmark up.Regent+Rose

Client
Regent+Rose
Industry
Beauty & Skincare
Services
Brand Identity, Packaging, Art Direction, E-commerce

Regent+Rose is a Miami-based skincare brand built around a simple daily ritual: Day and Night essentials powered by ingredients like propolis, royal jelly, centella and snow mushroom.

What we did

We designed the brand from the wordmark up — a bold, scalable identity system and a ‘+R’ monogram — and carried it across a complete packaging range, from gel cleanser to peptide moisturiser.

Art direction for product and lifestyle photography set the tone for social and the e-commerce site, with a Day and Night colour world that makes the range easy to navigate.
