48 hours of sports wonder at the Festival of Innovation Exchange 2024.Wonderland

The brief

The Innovation Exchange Festival presents: 48 hours of sports wonder. For Decathlon’s 2024 Festival of Innovation Exchange, the brief was to create a world that would pull teams out of the everyday and into a more playful, imaginative frame of mind.

What we did

We built Wonderland — a fluid, dreamlike identity spanning key visuals, posters, wayfinding and signage.

The world extended into a companion festival app and large-format screens, guiding attendees through workshops, talks and daily meditations across the event.
