Game Time at the House of amika — the identity and world for amika’s annual conference.amika

Client
amika
Industry
Beauty & Haircare
Services
Event Identity, Stage & LED Environment, Presentation System, Merch, Brand Playbook
Year
2026

amika:connect is amika’s first amika-only company conference — a moment to come together, reflect, share ideas, celebrate progress and align around what comes next.

For February 2026 in San Antonio, the theme was “Game Time at the House of amika” — a Super Bowl-inspired signal that amika now plays at the top level.

What we did

We built the whole conference world: a flexible amika:connect lockup, gradient key visuals, dot-matrix GAMEDAY typography, stage and LED environments, idle-screen states and lobby LED loops.

The system carried through two mainstage presentation decks, name badges and merch — jerseys numbered with each employee’s start year, Accolades varsity jackets and custom patches — all documented in a brand playbook.
