An innovation framework to move people through the wonders of sport.Design Égalité

Client
Decathlon
Industry
Sports, E-commerce
Services
Service Design, Identity Design, Design Strategy
In partnership with
SKEWED

Decathlon partnered with SKEWED to help define a new internal framework for innovation through the lens of Design Égalité.

The challenge was to create a compelling methodology that could align teams around the cultural importance of sports, movement and design while encouraging a more experimental, future-facing approach to product and retail thinking. Rather than focusing on a single outcome, the project explored how storytelling, systems thinking and immersive experiences could become tools for driving long-term innovation across the business.

What we did

BS supported SKEWED across the strategic and creative development of the initiative, helping shape the experience narrative, visual storytelling and presentation framework used to communicate the methodology internally.

Together, we developed a five-step innovation model designed to guide teams through ideation, validation and rollout, alongside a toolbox covering process, collaboration and implementation strategy.

The result was a scalable internal design framework that positioned Design Égalité as a catalyst for future innovation across Decathlon.
